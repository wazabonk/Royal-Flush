BLACKJACK · QUICK GUIDE

THE GOAL
Finish closer to 21 than the dealer without going over. Number cards count as shown, face cards count as 10, and Aces count as 1 or 11.
HIT
Take another card. If your total goes above 21, the hand busts and loses.
STAND
Keep your current total. The dealer then reveals the hidden card and plays.
DOUBLE
Double your wager, receive exactly one more card, then stand. Only available on the first two cards.
SPLIT
When your first two cards have the same value, split them into two hands with a second equal wager.
ALL IN
Sets the next hand's bet to your available virtual chip balance. It is a wager preset, not an extra action after the deal.
